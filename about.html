How We Evaluate Gear

We evaluate and compare guitars, amplifiers, pedals, and accessories using a standardized 5-point rubric combining manufacturer tolerances, verified street pricing, long-term owner reliability data, and hands-on playability benchmarks:

  • 1. Out-of-the-Box Setup & Playability: We examine neck profile comfort, factory string action, nut slotting accuracy, and fret-edge finish.
  • 2. Tonewoods & Electronics Integrity: From solid vs. laminated spruce tops on acoustics to Alnico/ceramic pickup voicings and pot/switch shielding on electrics.
  • 3. Tuning Stability & Hardware: Machine head gear ratios, bridge/saddle construction, and tremolo return accuracy.
  • 4. Street Price vs. Class Benchmarks: Every guitar is scored directly against its closest price-tier rivals (e.g., Yamaha FG800 vs. Fender CD-60S, or PRS SE Silver Sky vs. Fender Player Stratocaster).
  • 5. Long-Term Value & Resale: Build durability, humidity stability, and brand track record.
