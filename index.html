FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is the best guitar for a total beginner in 2026?

For acoustic players, we almost always recommend a solid-spruce top dreadnought like the Yamaha FG800. For electric, a HSS Strat-style guitar offers the most versatility for a learner.

How often should I change my strings for optimal tone?

In 2026, modern coated strings (like Elixir or D'Addario XS) can last 3-5 months for recreational players. However, session players should still change them every 15-20 hours of play for maximum brightness.

Are online guitar courses as good as in-person lessons?

Yes, and often they are more structured. Modern AI-assisted apps and HD video courses provide better visual angles and the ability to slow down complex riffs that a single teacher might miss.
