The Best Way to Distribute Your Guitar Music in 2026

Reviewed by George M. (Lead Guitar Reviewer)·✓ Fact-Checked & Price-Verified for 2026·How We Test

You've recorded your riffs, mastered your tracks, and now it's time to get your music on Spotify, Apple Music, and everywhere else. Here's how to do it.

In 2026, you don't need a record label to get your music heard. Independent music distribution platforms have leveled the playing field, allowing guitarists, bands, and solo artists to upload their tracks directly to major streaming services. But with so many options, which one is right for your specific needs?

After testing the top platforms, we've broken down the two absolute best options for guitarists depending on where you are in your musical journey: DistroKid and TuneCore.

1. DistroKid (Best for Frequent Releasers)

If you are an artist who writes and records frequently—whether it's acoustic fingerstyle covers, metal shred sessions, or full EP releases—DistroKid is our top recommendation. Their model is built on a flat annual fee, meaning you can upload unlimited songs without paying per release.

  • Pricing: Flat annual fee (no per-release costs).
  • Speed: Fastest upload to Spotify (often within 24-48 hours).
  • Royalties: You keep 100% of your earnings.
  • Extras: Automated splitting of royalties if you collaborate with other musicians.

2. TuneCore (Best for Career Building)

If you are building a serious brand and want more than just distribution, TuneCore is a powerhouse. While they now offer unlimited plans, their real strength lies in their publishing administration and synchronization pitching—meaning they actively help you get your guitar tracks into movies, TV shows, and video games.

  • Publishing Admin: Collect worldwide mechanical royalties.
  • Career Tools: Access to the TuneCore Accelerator program.
  • Royalties: Keep 100% of streaming revenue (depending on plan).
Explore TuneCore

The Verdict

If you just want to get your tracks online fast and cheap, go with DistroKid. If you are looking for long-term publishing administration to collect complex royalties, TuneCore is worth the investment.
