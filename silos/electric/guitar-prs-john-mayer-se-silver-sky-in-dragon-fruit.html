PRS JOHN MAYER SE SILVER SKY REVIEW 2026

The Dragon Fruit Identity: A 2026 Retrospective

The PRS SE Silver Sky in Dragon Fruit is more than just a vibrant finish; it represents the democratization of elite tone. Re-evaluating this model in 2026, we find its technical precision holds up against even the premium American counterparts.

Pickup Analysis: The 635JM "S" pickups offer a slightly higher output than the US models, making them more versatile for modern rock while retaining the John Mayer "glass" on the clean channel.
