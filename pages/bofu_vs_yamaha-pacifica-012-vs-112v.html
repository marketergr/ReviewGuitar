Yamaha Pacifica 012 vs 112V: Which to Buy?

Both are entry-level Pacificas, but the 112V is a meaningful step up from the budget 012. If you can stretch the extra, here's what you get for it.

Body and build

The 112V uses a solid alder body and more refined construction, while the cheaper 012 uses a more basic body wood. The alder body of the 112V contributes to a fuller, more resonant tone.

Pickups and wiring

The 112V adds upgraded pickups with a coil-split on the humbucker for extra tonal options, plus a vintage-style tremolo. The 012 has simpler pickups and electronics with fewer sounds on tap.

Hardware and feel

The 112V's better tuners, bridge, and overall fit-and-finish make it noticeably nicer to play and keep in tune. The 012 is fine for a first guitar, but you'll likely feel the difference quickly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Yamaha Pacifica 112V worth more than the 012?

For most players, yes. The 112V's solid alder body, upgraded pickups with coil-split, vintage tremolo, and better hardware make it a noticeably more capable and better-sounding guitar. The 012 is a fine entry point, but the 112V is the one most players won't outgrow as quickly.

What's the biggest difference between the 012 and 112V?

The 112V offers better tonewood (solid alder), upgraded pickups with a coil-split for more sounds, a vintage-style tremolo, and improved hardware. The 012 keeps things basic to hit a lower price. The 112V simply does more and feels more refined.

Is the cheaper 012 good enough for a beginner?

It's perfectly playable as a first guitar, but many beginners find they want the 112V's extra tonal flexibility and nicer feel within a year. If budget is tight the 012 will get you started; if you can stretch, the 112V is the better long-term buy.
