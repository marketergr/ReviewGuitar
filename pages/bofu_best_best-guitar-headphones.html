Choosing Headphones for Silent Amp Practice

Practising through your amp's headphone output (or a modeling amp/interface) lets you play full-volume tones at any hour. The right headphones make that sound great; the wrong ones make it tiring. Here's what matters.

Closed-back for isolation

Closed-back headphones keep sound in and outside noise out, which is exactly what you want for silent practice. Open-back models sound airy but leak sound, defeating the point of practising quietly.

Accurate, full-range sound

Studio-style monitor headphones reproduce your amp tone honestly, with clear highs and solid low end so palm mutes and distortion feel real. Overly 'hyped' consumer headphones can mask what you actually sound like.

Comfort for long sessions

You'll wear these for hours, so look for comfortable padding and a manageable clamp. Swivel cups and replaceable pads are a bonus that extends their life.

Connection to your amp

Most amps use a 1/4" headphone jack, while many headphones terminate in 1/8" (3.5mm) — a cheap adapter bridges the gap. Check your amp's output so you have the right connector on hand.

Frequently Asked Questions

Open-back or closed-back headphones for guitar practice?

Closed-back, in almost every case. They isolate sound so you can practise silently without disturbing others and without outside noise bleeding in. Open-back headphones sound more spacious but leak sound badly, which defeats the purpose of quiet practice.

Can I plug headphones straight into my amp?

Yes, if your amp has a headphone output — most modeling and practice amps do. Just match the connector: amps typically use a 1/4" jack, so you may need a 1/4"-to-1/8" adapter for headphones with a smaller plug. A dedicated headphone amp is only needed if your amp lacks a headphone out.

Do I need expensive studio headphones?

Not necessarily, but accurate closed-back monitor headphones are worth it for practice because they show your tone honestly and stay comfortable for long sessions. Mid-priced studio monitors like the popular closed-back models hit the sweet spot of sound, durability, and value.
