Yamaha FG800 vs Fender CD-60S: How to Choose

These are the two most-recommended beginner dreadnoughts, and they're closely matched. The decision comes down to feel and tone preference, not quality — both are outstanding value with a solid top.

Tone character

The FG800 is known for a bright, projecting, slightly scooped voice; the CD-60S leans a touch warmer and rounder. Neither is 'better' — it's about which sound suits your music and your ear.

Neck feel

The CD-60S has rolled fingerboard edges and an easy-rolling neck many beginners find instantly comfortable. The FG800's neck is a hair chunkier. If you can, hold both before deciding.

Build and solid top

Both pair a solid spruce top with laminate back and sides — exactly the right build at this price — so both will open up and sound richer as they age.

Which to pick

Choose the FG800 for bright strumming projection and Yamaha's legendary consistency; choose the CD-60S for a warmer voice and an extra-comfortable neck straight out of the box.

Is the Yamaha FG800 or Fender CD-60S better for beginners?
