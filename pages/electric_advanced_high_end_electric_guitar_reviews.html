Getting Started with Tone

Your first electric guitar should be versatile and easy to handle. For the electric guitars, we look for lightweight bodies and necks that feel slim in your hand. Don't worry about all the knobs and switches just yet—modern designs in 2026 are built to sound great "out of the box." We've picked options that stay in tune even when you're just learning to bend notes for the first time.

Our Helpful Verdict

In the end, the best choice is the one that keeps you playing. In 2026, the quality of beginner and intermediate gear is at an all-time high, and we're confident that any of these picks will be a great partner for your musical journey.

Is this hard to get started with as a total beginner?

Not at all! In fact, most of the gear in 2026 is designed specifically to be accessible and easy to use. You'll be surprised how quickly you can get a great sound.

Do I need to spend a lot right away?

Definitely not. We've found some amazing "Budget Gems" that perform way above their price tag. It's more about finding the right fit for your hands than the most expensive label.
