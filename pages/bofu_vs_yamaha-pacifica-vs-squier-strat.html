Yamaha Pacifica 112V vs Squier Classic Vibe Strat

Two of the best sub-$400 electrics for beginners and gigging players on a budget. The Pacifica 112V and the Squier Classic Vibe Stratocaster are both superb value — here's how they differ.

Pickups and versatility

The Pacifica 112V has an HSS layout (a bridge humbucker plus two single-coils) with a coil-split, so it covers everything from Strat sparkle to thicker rock tones. The Classic Vibe Strat is a traditional three-single-coil setup for authentic, classic Stratocaster voices.

Tone and authenticity

If you want the genuine, glassy Strat sound and Fender feel, the Classic Vibe nails it. If you want one guitar that does more styles, the Pacifica's humbucker makes it the more versatile all-rounder.

Build and value

Both are famous for over-delivering at the price with solid hardware and good fretwork. The Pacifica is renowned for bulletproof consistency; the Classic Vibe adds vintage-correct details and the Fender headstock many players want.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Yamaha Pacifica 112V or Squier Classic Vibe Strat more versatile?

The Pacifica 112V is more versatile thanks to its HSS pickup layout and coil-split, which let it cover both single-coil sparkle and thicker humbucker rock tones. The Classic Vibe Strat is a pure three-single-coil design that excels at classic Strat sounds but covers less ground.

Which has a more authentic Stratocaster tone?

The Squier Classic Vibe Stratocaster, by design. Its three single-coils and vintage-correct voicing deliver the genuine glassy, quacky Strat tones, plus the Fender-family feel and headstock. The Pacifica sounds great but its humbucker gives it a slightly different, more versatile character.

Which is better for a beginner?

Both are excellent first guitars. Choose the Pacifica 112V if you want maximum tonal flexibility from one guitar, or the Classic Vibe Strat if you specifically want that iconic Stratocaster sound and feel. Build quality is outstanding on both at the price.
