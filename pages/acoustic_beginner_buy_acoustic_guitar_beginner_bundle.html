  • Mistake #1 — Buying unbranded "all-in-one" stencil kits under $100: Ultra-cheap off-brand instruments use soft pot-metal frets, unseasoned necks that warp within 60 days, and high string action that makes learning painful. Stick to proven builders (Yamaha FG800J, Yamaha FG830, Fender CD-60S, Alvarez AF30CE Artist Series).
  • Mistake #2 — Ignoring setup geometry and string gauge: Even a great guitar feels stiff if shipped with heavy strings or a high nut slot. Always verify string action height (1.6mm–2.0mm at 12th fret) during your 30-day return window.
