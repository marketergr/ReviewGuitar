Martin LX1 vs Baby Taylor: Best Small-Body Travel Guitar

The Little Martin (LX1) and the Baby Taylor (BT1) are the two most popular small-scale travel guitars, perfect for travel, couches, kids, and small hands. Here's how they compare.

Tone

The Martin LX1, with its solid spruce top, leans warm and full with a surprisingly rich low end for its size. The Baby Taylor is bright, articulate, and crisp — the classic Taylor voice in a compact body.

Size and feel

Both are roughly 3/4-scale and very portable. The Baby Taylor's neck is famously easy and beginner-friendly; the LX1 feels a little more substantial and 'Martin' in the hand.

Build and use

Both are tough, travel-ready, and excellent for kids, small hands, and practice anywhere. Choose the LX1 for warmth and Martin character, or the Baby Taylor for brightness and an easy neck.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Martin LX1 or Baby Taylor better for travel?

Both are excellent travel guitars — compact, tough, and great-sounding for their size. The LX1 offers a warmer, fuller Martin tone, while the Baby Taylor is brighter with an especially easy neck. Choose based on the sound you prefer; for portability they're very close.

Which is better for a child or small hands?

Both are ideal thanks to their roughly 3/4-scale size and comfortable necks. The Baby Taylor's slim, easy neck is often praised for younger or smaller-handed players, while the Little Martin is equally manageable and adds a warmer tone. Either works wonderfully as a first or travel guitar.

Do these small guitars sound full enough?

For their size, yes — both produce a surprisingly full sound, especially the solid-top LX1 with its warm low end. They won't match a full dreadnought's volume and bass, but as travel and practice guitars they sound far bigger than you'd expect.
