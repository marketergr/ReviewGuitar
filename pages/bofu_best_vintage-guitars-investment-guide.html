Investing in Vintage Guitars: What to Buy in 2026

Vintage guitars have consistently outperformed the stock market over the last two decades. But in 2026, the landscape has changed. Pre-CBS Fenders and '59 Les Pauls are essentially museum pieces, so where should the smart money go?

Reverb.com is the undisputed king of the vintage market. It offers buyer protection and a global inventory that local pawn shops simply cannot match.

The "Player Grade" 70s Fenders

While 60s Fenders are astronomically priced, the mid-to-late 70s models are seeing a massive resurgence in value. Look for hardtail Stratocasters and Telecaster Customs with original Wide-Range Humbuckers.

Early 90s PRS Guitars

Paul Reed Smith guitars manufactured before 1995 (specifically in the old Annapolis factory) are highly sought after by collectors. They feature smaller heel joints and highly figured tops that are incredibly rare today.
