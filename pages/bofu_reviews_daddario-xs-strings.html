Are D'Addario XS Coated Strings Worth the Price?

XS is D'Addario's premium coated string, promising long life without the dull, slick feel that gave older coated strings a bad name. Here's whether the premium is justified.

The coating technology

An ultra-thin coating shields the strings from the sweat, dirt, and corrosion that kill tone, aiming to keep them bright and alive far longer than an uncoated set.

Longevity versus cost

Coated strings cost more per set but can last several times longer, so for players whose strings die quickly the real cost-per-week often works out lower — plus far fewer restringing sessions.

Tone and feel

XS retains more high-end sparkle than older coated strings and feels smooth without the squeaky, plasticky drag some players dislike. For most ears it sounds close to uncoated when fresh.

Who benefits most

Players with acidic sweat that eats strings, gigging musicians who can't restring often, and anyone who simply hates the chore of frequent string changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do D'Addario XS strings actually last?

Many players report several times the usable life of uncoated strings — often weeks to months longer before the tone goes dead. The exact gain depends on your body chemistry and how often you play, but corrosion-prone players see the biggest difference.

Do coated strings sound different from regular strings?

When fresh, modern coatings like XS sound very close to uncoated strings — some ears notice a touch less initial zing. The real advantage is that they hold that fresh tone much longer, so a month in they often sound clearly better than a dead uncoated set.

Are XS strings worth the higher price?

If your strings go dead quickly or you can't restring often, yes — the longer life lowers your real cost and saves restringing time. If you already change strings every week for that brand-new feel, cheaper uncoated sets make more sense.
