What specifications matter most when comparing Bass Guitars?

Focus on 34-inch long scale vs 30-inch short scale, split single-coil Precision (P-Bass) vs dual single-coil Jazz (J-Bass) pickups, 38mm slim C-neck nut width, Alnico V punch, passive volume/tone wiring vs active 3-band EQ, as well as real-world setup benchmarks like a 30-inch short scale (for smaller hands or guitarists crossing over) vs a 34-inch standard scale with 2.4mm action at the 12th fret. These factors impact playability and tone far more than cosmetic finishes.
