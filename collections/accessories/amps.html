⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid When Shopping for Top-Rated Guitar Amps Review 2026

  • Daisy-chaining digital loopers and analog overdrive pedals on a cheap wall wart: Mixing digital and analog pedals without isolated power introduces a high-pitched whine into your amp.
  • Putting time-based/looper pedals before distortion: Place your tuner and wah first, overdrive/distortion (like the Boss BD-2 and DS-1) in the middle, and your TC Electronic Ditto Looper at the very end of the chain so loops don't get muddied.
  • Using non-nitro-safe foam guitar stands on vintage/Gibson finishes: Standard black neoprene rubber can react with nitrocellulose lacquer; use Hercules AGS or String Swing nitro-safe padding.
  • Waiting until strings rust or break to replace them: Coated strings like D'Addario XS hold intonation and harmonic clarity 3–4x longer than uncoated sets.
