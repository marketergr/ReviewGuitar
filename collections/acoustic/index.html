What specifications matter most when comparing Acoustic Guitars?

Focus on solid Sitka spruce top, scalloped X-bracing, rosewood vs mahogany back and sides, bone nut and saddle, 43mm nut width, dreadnought vs concert OM body, Fishman and LR Baggs acoustic-electric preamps, as well as real-world setup benchmarks like a 12th-fret string action of 2.2mm on the low E and 1.8mm on the high E with .012–.053 phosphor bronze strings. These factors impact playability and tone far more than cosmetic finishes.
