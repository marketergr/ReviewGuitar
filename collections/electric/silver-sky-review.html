⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid When Shopping for Prs John Mayer Se Silver Sky Review 2026

  • Choosing a cheap locking/Floyd Rose tremolo as a beginner: Budget floating tremolos detune constantly during string bends and make drop-D tuning frustrating; stick to a hardtail or vintage 2-point tremolo first.
  • Mismatching pickup configuration to your genre: Traditional single-coils (SSS) nail Mayer/Hendrix cleans but hum under heavy metal gain; an HSS Strat/Pacifica or dual-humbucker (HH) SG/Les Paul covers rock and high-gain effortlessly.
  • Spending 90% of the budget on the guitar and 10% on a tiny 5W practice amp: Even a $1,500 Fender sounds boxy through a 4-inch speaker; pair your guitar with a modeling amp like the Boss Katana-50.
  • Neglecting fret-end dressing and nut slotting: A quick luthier setup and graphite nut lubrication make a $300 Squier or Yamaha play like an instrument twice its price.
