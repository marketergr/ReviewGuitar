Expert Verdict: Which should you choose?

While both instruments are excellent, they target different players. The **Gibson Les Paul Standard '60s** is an investment in tone and craft, featuring higher-tier materials that will only improve with age. The **Squier Affinity Telecaster**, on the other hand, is the ultimate workhorse—reliable, punchy, and priced perfectly for the gigging musician who needs a bulletproof tool. For the absolute best sound regardless of cost, go with the Gibson Les Paul Standard '60s. For the best ROI, the Squier Affinity Telecaster wins every time.
